# Data-quality report: first refreshed snapshot (#73)

This is the closing report for #73: the first snapshot taken with the scrapers in `scripts/scrape/`. The per-run counts generated by `scripts/refresh_seed.py` are in [`report.md`](report.md); this report reads them against the targets in the product and technical specifications. No collection date is recorded or shown (PR-06).

## How the snapshot was taken

| Retailer | Published listings | Search for missing listings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FairPrice | Every page read again (257 listings) | 37 products searched, up to 3 hits each |
| Cold Storage | Every page read again (189 listings) | 105 products searched, up to 3 hits each |
| RedMart | **Carried over** unchanged from the previous seed (177 listings) | 14 of 117 products searched, best hit only; **103 skipped** |

Lazada answered scripted reads with its slider check and Google reCAPTCHA, often on the first page. The product owner therefore decided to carry RedMart's published listings over rather than read them again, and to search only for the RedMart listings the catalogue was missing. When the check budget (`--max-checks`) ran out, the remaining RedMart searches were skipped. A skipped search contributes no candidates and is never filled from earlier data. RedMart prices in this seed are therefore those of the previous snapshot, which the fixed-dataset disclaimer covers.

## Matching

Search hits first had to pass the name rules: the same pack count and per-pack size, the brand's word and a similar name. An AI reviewer then judged each one with the Stage 2F rubric. Only MATCH with HIGH confidence publishes (PR-02).

| Retailer | Candidates | MATCH (high) | NOT_MATCH | UNSURE |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| FairPrice | 30 | 20 | 9 | 1 |
| Cold Storage | 17 | 13 | 3 | 1 |
| RedMart | 11 | 9 | 0 | 2 |
| **Total** | **58** | **42** | **12** | **4** |

- **Rejections:** NOT_MATCH decisions were all different variants of the same brand and size, such as low fat, a different flavour or scent, less sugar, or plant sterols.
- **Uncertain matches:** UNSURE cases had evidence that didn't settle identity, such as "Traditional Hard Cheese" for Parmesan, a 3 L name with a 2.8 L link, or a formulation the other retailers don't state. None of them published.
- **New listings:** 37 were published. The other 5 accepted candidates were out of stock at Cold Storage: the two Kodomo detergents, Marigold Greek-style yoghurt, Marigold HL strawberry milk and Shokubutsu Lumi Glow body wash.
- **Limit:** no independent human sample was drawn for this snapshot. The AI decisions are recorded in `match-decisions.csv` for review.

## Sellers and fulfilment

- **New RedMart listings:** all 11 candidates were read from their product pages, and every one showed RedMart as the seller. No third-party seller was found, and no seller was unreadable, so no hand check was needed.
- **Carried-over RedMart listings:** these keep the seller evidence of the previous seed (the Stage 2H checks), not a fresh reading.

## Prices and promotions

- **Multibuys:** 115 listings carry an applied same-product multibuy, and 55 carry a promotion shown as not included (mostly "Spend $X + free gift").
- **Reference checker:** the committed-seed integration test prices every listing at 1–20 packs and matches an independent calculation. It passes on this seed in `pnpm check`.
- **Checkout evidence:** PR-03's check of about 10 deals per retailer against the live checkout has **not been done**. It needs a person in a browser. Until then, the half-up rounding of percentage deals is unverified.

## Shortfalls

1. **RedMart was not refreshed.** RedMart prices, stock and promotions are those of the previous snapshot, by product owner decision, because of Lazada's anti-bot checks.
2. **Most missing RedMart listings weren't searched.** 103 of 117 searches were skipped, including Ayam Brand Sardines in Tomato Sauce 155g, the QA item that prompted #73, so that gap remains. Resuming the RedMart search, which continues from its checkpoint, is the way to close it.
3. **The catalogue shrank to 238 products,** mostly because of stock at the time of the snapshot. Retired products return automatically when they are back in stock at two retailers.
4. **Live checkout evidence for deals is outstanding** (PR-03).
